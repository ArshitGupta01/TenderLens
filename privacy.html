Privacy

TenderLens is a hackathon project. This notice explains what happens when you use its sample website and optional live search.

Using the sample

Searching sample tenders, changing filters, saving an example, using the checklist and opening a sample report all happen in your browser. These actions do not send your profile or selections to a server. They reset when you reload the page. Your light or dark theme choice is saved in this browser so it stays the same on your next visit.

Choosing live search

Live search is optional. After you press “Search live tenders,” the page checks whether the TenderLens service is available. If it is ready, the search words and the portal you chose are sent to the configured service, which asks TinyFish for public tender notices. The website does not send your sample checklist or bidder profile with this search.

The search provider and the server that runs TenderLens may process ordinary connection details needed to answer the request. The website does not save searches to a user account or bidder profile. Results may be incomplete or out of date; open the official notice before relying on it.

Files and company information

This website does not upload tender files or company records. The backend also contains early developer routes for fetching a tender and making a sample scorecard, but this page does not call those routes. Do not send confidential files or business information to an experimental server.

Other services

The website may load fonts from Google Fonts. Lenis, the smooth-scrolling library, is included with the project and runs locally. The site does not use advertising pixels or analytics tools.

Questions

This preview is presented by Team Traxo for HackIIITD. A support email address has not been provided.
