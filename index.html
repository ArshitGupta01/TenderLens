    08 / QUICK ANSWERS

    A few things to know.

    New to TenderLens? These answers explain how the sample and account features work.

    Are the tenders and results real?

    No. Tender names, buyers, dates, amounts and report findings on this page are made-up examples.

    Can I check if my company qualifies?

    Not yet. The checklist is only a local demo. This website does not accept company files or run a real eligibility review.

    What happens if I use live search?

    Live search works only when the optional service is configured. When you submit, it sends your search words and selected portal to that service. Open official notices to confirm every detail.

    Is my bidder profile sent to TenderLens?

    A signed-out draft stays on this device. If you sign in and choose “Save profile,” the basic details are stored in your private account so they can sync across devices. The profile is never included in live searches. “Clear local draft” removes only this browser's copy.

    Can I save a tender for later?

    Yes. After a live search returns a genuine notice, sign in and choose “Save to account.” Fictional sample tenders and their bookmarks stay in this browser.

    Where can I learn how to use the page?

    Open the easy guide for a short walkthrough, glossary, search setup and privacy details.
