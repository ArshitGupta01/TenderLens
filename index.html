    02 / THE PROBLEMA tender is rarely one document.

    The hard part comes after discovery

    The clause that changes everything may be in the third file.

    A main bid can point to an uploaded ATC. A later corrigendum can revise that ATC again. The planned TenderLens workflow brings the document set together, tracks what changed, and compares it with a bidder profile.

    01

    Main bid

    Baseline thresholds, EMD, timelines and standard terms.

    02

    Buyer-added ATC

    Specific experience, certificates and OEM conditions.

    03

    Corrigenda

    Amendments, overrides and the terms that now apply.

    06 / BUILT FOR ACCOUNTABILITY

    Confidence comes from knowing why.

    A

    Every finding points to a source

    Document name, page and clause stay attached to the requirement and its status.

    B

    Newer terms are considered in context

    Corrigenda are checked against the earlier bid and ATC, with superseded language preserved in the trail.

    C

    Uncertainty stays visible

    Conflicting or incomplete evidence is flagged for review. The system supports a bid decision; it does not make a legal determination.

    07 / WHO IT IS FOR

    Made for the people who carry the bid risk.
