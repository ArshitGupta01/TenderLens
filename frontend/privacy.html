Privacy

TenderLens is a hackathon project. This notice explains what happens when you use its sample pages, optional live search and account features.

Using the sample

Sample searches, filters, fictional saved examples, the checklist and report controls run in your browser. Your light or dark theme choice and a signed-out bidder-profile draft are saved on this device. Fictional tender examples and checklist selections are not uploaded or stored in your account.

Accounts and saved live tenders

When account sign-in is enabled, Supabase Auth handles your email address, password credentials, confirmation and session. TenderLens does not receive or store your password. If you sign up with Google, Google and Supabase process the sign-in under their own terms. Supabase stores the basic profile fields you choose to save: name, organization, bidder type, years in business and operating region. Your saved live tender links include the notice title, official URL, portal name and search snippet. These details are stored in Supabase Postgres and protected by row-level security so an account can read or change only its own rows.

You can edit your profile or remove individual saved tenders from your account. Account deletion is not currently available as a self-service action. Avoid entering registration numbers, financial figures, bank details, bid documents or other confidential information.

Choosing live search

Live search is optional. After you press “Search live tenders,” the page checks whether the TenderLens service is available. If it is ready, only the search words and the portal you chose are sent to the configured service, which asks TinyFish for public tender notices. The website does not send your local bidder profile, checklist or saved examples with this search.

The search provider and the server that runs TenderLens may process ordinary connection details needed to answer the request. Search phrases are not saved to a TenderLens account. A live result is stored only if you sign in and explicitly choose “Save to account.” Results may be incomplete or out of date; open the official notice before relying on them.

Strategy preview and public-source search

When you choose “Create review preview,” the bidder and tender values entered on the Strategy tools page are sent to the configured FastAPI service to create a response. That preview route does not save the form values. Avoid entering confidential or identifying financial records. If you separately choose “Search public sources,” the tender title and selected portal are sent to the TenderLens service, which may query its configured public-search provider. Returned pages are unverified search results, not a verified history of bid outcomes.

Files and company information

This website does not upload tender files or company records. A local profile draft stays in your browser. If you are signed in, choosing “Save profile” sends only the basic profile fields described above to Supabase so they can sync between your devices. This saved profile is not automatically sent to the Strategy tools preview or an eligibility review.

Other services

The website may load fonts from Google Fonts and the Supabase JavaScript client from jsDelivr. Lenis, the smooth-scrolling library, is included with the project and runs locally. The site does not use advertising pixels or analytics tools.

Questions
