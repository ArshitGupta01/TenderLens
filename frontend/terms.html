A PLAIN-LANGUAGE NOTICE · UPDATED 24 OCTOBER 2026

Terms & conditions

These terms apply to the TenderLens hackathon website, optional live search and account features. TenderLens is an early prototype, not a bidding or legal service.

Sample information

The sample tenders, buyers, values, dates, company profile and review results are fictional. They are included only to show how the website could work. Do not treat a sample status as a real eligibility result.

Live tender search

If the live search service is running, you can choose to send a search phrase and a portal to it. Search results may be incomplete, old or incorrect. Always open the official notice and check the latest documents yourself.

Not legal or bid advice

TenderLens does not decide whether you should bid or whether you qualify. It does not guarantee that a tender is active, that every linked file was found, or that a requirement was understood correctly. Check the official documents and contact the buyer when something is unclear.

Prototype and account features

The sample tenders, report, filters, saved-example buttons and bidder checklist are illustrative. Sample results are not real eligibility decisions and sample saves stay in this browser for the current visit. When account sign-in is configured, Supabase Auth creates and manages your login. Supabase Postgres stores only the basic bidder profile you choose to save and live notice links that you explicitly save. Row-level security limits each account to its own records. Do not enter confidential business information.

You are responsible for checking every official notice and deciding whether to bid. TenderLens does not confirm that a saved notice is still active. You can edit your profile or remove saved tender links from your account. Account deletion is not yet a self-service feature.

External providers

When you choose live search, the configured TenderLens server may use TinyFish to find public notices. Account sign-in and stored account data are provided by Supabase. Google sign-in also uses Google. Those providers have their own terms and privacy practices. The site loads the Supabase browser client from jsDelivr.

Using this preview

Use this website to explore the project and its sample interface. Do not rely on it for a purchase, bid, compliance decision or legal conclusion. The prototype is provided as-is while the team continues development.

Project contact

TenderLens is presented by Team Traxo for HackIIITD. No support address or commercial service has been provided.
