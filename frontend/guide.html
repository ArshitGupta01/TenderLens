02 / DOCUMENT HIERARCHY

How Indian legal precedence works

In Indian administrative law, contradictory terms across tender PDFs are resolved by strict hierarchical order. TenderLens constructs a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) to enforce this deterministically:

Launch DAG Workbench ↗
RANK 1

Corrigenda & Official Amendments

Governing authority over all prior documents. Any change to dates, turnover criteria, or EMD terms immediately negates earlier clauses.

↓ overrides
RANK 2

GeM General Terms & Conditions (GTC)

Statutory baseline for all public buying. Protects statutory exemptions (Clauses 4(xiii)(h), 4(xxii)) against buyer violations.

↓ overrides
RANK 3

Buyer Additional Terms & Conditions (ATC)

Custom buyer requirements. Valid only if they do NOT conflict with GeM GTC or Government of India public procurement policies.

↓ overrides
RANK 4

Consortium & Joint Venture Frameworks

Defines prime bidder and subcontractor role distributions, split-turnover aggregations, and technical responsibilities.

↓ overrides
RANK 5

Main RFP / Tender Notice

Baseline initial tender scope. Completely superseded whenever an amendment or corrigendum is issued.

04 / ZERO-HISTORY STARTUP EXEMPTION

How to bid on ₹10Cr+ tenders with ₹0 revenue

If your company is under 10 years old and registered with DPIIT, Indian public procurement law gives you statutory exemptions from prior turnover and experience.

Test in Strategy Studio ↗
  1. GFR 2017 Rule 173(i) Waiver: Ministry of Finance mandates that prior turnover and prior experience are waived for recognized startups, subject to technical specifications.
  2. Technical Equivalence over Past Revenue: Instead of past balance sheets, provide government-accredited lab test reports (NABL, BIS, STQC) proving your product meets specifications.
  3. Annexure-I Quality Control Proforma: TenderLens automatically drafts the official equipment and quality assurance affidavit that protects the government buyer from audit inquiries.
  4. Pre-Bid Legal Representation: If a buyer attempts to write "No startup exemption" in the fine print, TenderLens auto-drafts a formal challenge letter citing GeM GTC Clause 4(xiii)(h) to remove the unfair barrier.
Statutory Ground: Ministry of Finance Department of Expenditure OM F.20/2/2014-PPD(Pt.) explicitly prohibits procuring entities from diluting or denying startup exemptions in public tenders.

05 / MANDATORY 25% MSE SUBCONTRACTING

Claim the mandatory 25% MSE subcontracting quota on mega tenders

Even when a tender is too large (e.g. ₹50 Crore) for a small firm to bid as prime contractor, the law guarantees you a 25% share.

Calculate Your 25% Quota ↗
  1. The ₹2 Crore Mandatory Trigger: Under Section 4 of the Public Procurement Policy for MSEs Order 2012, any Central Government procurement valued at or above ₹200 Lakhs (₹2 Crore) mandates that the winning prime contractor must subcontract at least 25% of the total order value to registered Micro & Small Enterprises.
  2. L1 + 15% Split-Order Purchase Preference: When an MSE quotes within 15% of the lowest non-MSE bid (L1), the MSE has the statutory right to match L1's price and win 25% of the order quantity.
  3. Ready-to-Send Partnership Outreach: TenderLens auto-generates a formal tripartite partnership agreement citing the statutory 25% quota to present to prime EPC bidders.
Debarment Protection: Prime contractors who fail to fulfill their 25% MSE subcontracting quota face contract cancellation and debarment under GeM GTC Clause 4(xxii).

06 / PROCUREMENT GLOSSARY

Statutory terms & definitions

Indian public procurement relies on specific statutory definitions. Here are the authoritative standards used in TenderLens.

Corrigendum
An official gazetted amendment that overrides, modifies, or cancels earlier tender terms. Has Rank 1 legal precedence.
ATC
Additional Terms and Conditions: custom rules specified by the buyer. Legally void if they contradict GeM GTC or GFR statutory exemptions.
GTC
General Terms & Conditions: the overarching platform contract governing all transactions on Government e-Marketplace (GeM).
GFR 2017
General Financial Rules, 2017: statutory procurement rules issued by the Ministry of Finance governing all Central Government expenditure.
Rule 170(i)
Statutory provision granting full Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) exemption to Micro & Small Enterprises and recognized Startups.
Rule 173(i)
Mandates that prior turnover and operating experience criteria must be relaxed for recognized Startups & MSEs.
Class-I Local Supplier
A supplier whose goods or services offer local value addition equal to or exceeding 50% under the DPIIT Make in India Order.
Insurance Surety Bond
An IRDAI-approved guarantee instrument permitted under GFR Rule 170(i) in lieu of costly bank guarantees and fixed deposits.
