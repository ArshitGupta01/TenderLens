02 / STRATEGY PREVIEW

What the new tools do

The preview uses tender and bidder values that you enter. It does not read tender files, save the submitted form, or make a legal or eligibility decision.

  1. Use clarification prompts to identify questions you may want to ask through the official tender channel.
  2. Review possible capability gaps. TenderLens does not list or verify consortium partners.
  3. Download the checklist as a CSV draft; entered values are not supporting evidence.
  4. Use the EMD figure as a cash scenario only. The tool does not confirm exemptions or permitted instruments.
  5. If you choose public-source search, the tender title and portal are sent to the configured search provider. Search results need manual verification.

04 / USEFUL WORDS

A short glossary

Tender documents use a lot of abbreviations. Here are the ones used on this site.

Tender
A buyer’s request for companies to offer goods, work or services.
ATC
“Additional Terms and Conditions”: extra rules added by the buyer.
Corrigendum
An official notice that changes or clarifies an earlier tender.
Clause
One numbered rule or paragraph inside a tender file.
Evidence
A document or record that helps show whether a requirement is met.
Bidder profile
Basic details about the business preparing a bid. Save a local draft or sync it to your signed-in account.

05 / ZERO-HISTORY STARTUP EXEMPTION

How to bid with zero financial history

If your company is under 10 years old and recognized by DPIIT, Indian public procurement law gives you statutory exemptions from prior turnover and experience.

Launch Strategy Studio & Shield ↗
  1. GFR 2017 Rule 173(i) Waiver: Ministry of Finance mandates that prior turnover and prior experience are waived for recognized startups, subject to technical specifications.
  2. Technical Equivalence over Past Revenue: Instead of past balance sheets, provide government-accredited lab test reports (NABL, BIS, STQC) proving your product meets specifications.
  3. Annexure-I Quality Control Proforma: TenderLens automatically drafts the official equipment and quality assurance affidavit that protects the government buyer from audit inquiries.
  4. Pre-Bid Legal Representation: If a buyer attempts to write "No startup exemption" in the fine print, TenderLens auto-drafts a formal challenge letter citing GeM GTC Clause 4(xiii)(h) to remove the unfair barrier.

06 / MANDATORY 25% MSE SUBCONTRACTING

Unlock guaranteed 25% subcontracts on mega tenders

Even when a tender is too large (e.g. ₹50 Crore) for a small firm to bid as prime contractor, the law guarantees you a 25% share.

Calculate Your 25% Quota ↗
  1. The ₹2 Crore Mandatory Trigger: Under Section 4 of the Public Procurement Policy for MSEs Order 2012, any Central Government procurement valued at or above ₹200 Lakhs (₹2 Crore) mandates that the winning prime contractor must subcontract at least 25% of the total order value to registered Micro & Small Enterprises.
  2. Carve-Out Package Identification: TenderLens analyzes the tender scope of work and automatically identifies non-core sub-packages (installation, commissioning, testing, component supply) suitable for small businesses.
  3. Ready-to-Send Partnership Outreach: TenderLens auto-generates a ready-to-mail formal partnership proposal citing the statutory 25% quota to present to prime EPC bidders.

Statutory Protection: Prime contractors who fail to fulfill their 25% MSE subcontracting quota face contract cancellation and debarment under GeM GTC Clause 4(xxii).
